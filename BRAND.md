# Vanitylab visual identity

Typeface: Manrope across the Mini App and brand wordmark. Use 400 for body, 500–600 for interface emphasis and 600 for headings and the Vanitylab wordmark. Telegram chat text uses the Telegram client font and cannot be overridden.

Symbol: approved asymmetric white V. Background: violet glow at upper right fading to near-black at the bottom, with visible fine grain restricted to the background.
